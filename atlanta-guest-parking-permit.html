How to get a guest parking permit in Atlanta

If you live in one of the City of Atlanta's residential permit parking zones, a visitor who parks on a street signed for permit parking needs a guest permit (some people call it a guest pass or a visitor parking permit). Guest permits are free and you create them online on the City's permit portal. This guide walks through it.

Last updated November 2026. This is not an official City of Atlanta page; where it and the City differ, the City is right.

The short version

  • Where: the City's permit portal, atlparkingpermits.rmcpay.com, signed in to your own residential permit account.
  • Cost: free.
  • How many: the City allows two active guest permits at a time.
  • How long: you choose 1 to 10 days, or 15 days.
  • Nothing to print: the permit is digital and tied to the car's license plate. There is no hang tag or paper pass for the dashboard.
  • The plate rule: type the digit 0 wherever the plate has the letter O. See why below.

What you need

  • A City of Atlanta residential parking permit account on the portal. If you don't have one yet, start at the City's Residential Permit Parking page, which links to the application and lists the annual permit fee.
  • Your guest's vehicle details: license plate number and state, make, model, and color.
  • The dates: the first day your guest will park and how many days they are staying.

Step by step process on the City's portal (or click HERE for the faster way)

  1. Sign in. Go to atlparkingpermits.rmcpay.com and enter the email address or phone number on your account. The first time you sign in on a phone or computer, the portal sends you a short verification code. After that it asks for the 4-digit PIN you chose when you set up the account.
  2. Start a new permit. On your account page choose Add New Permit. On the "Please select a permit" page, choose the guest permit.
  3. Enter your details. First and last name, address, city, zip code, phone number, and email. The form asks for these each time.
  4. Choose the permit details. Pick your Zone, the Cycle (the number of days), and the Valid From date. The start date can be today or a later date, so you can set up a permit ahead of a visit.
  5. Enter the vehicle. License plate state, license plate number, make, model, and color. Remember the 0-for-O rule.
  6. Acknowledge and agree. Type your name in the acknowledgment box and check the Terms box.
  7. Check availability, then check out. The form shows an Availability line. If it says the permit is available, choose Checkout.
  8. Confirm the amount is $0.00 and submit. The cart shows the amount due, which should be $0.00. Choose Submit. The confirmation shows the permit's dates, and the permit appears on your account page.

These are the steps as of November 2026. The City or its portal provider may change the wording or the order.

Rules worth knowing

Two guest permits at a time
The City allows two active guest permits per account. If two are already in effect on the days you ask for, the portal shows the permit as not available. Pick a start date after one of them ends.
You can't end a permit early
The portal offers no way to cancel or shorten a guest permit once it is issued. It runs through 11:59 PM on its last day, and it holds one of your two slots until then. Choose the number of days with that in mind.
Enter 0 for every letter O
Residential parking is enforced with license plate readers, and the City says they do not recognize the letter O. The City tells residents to enter the digit 0 instead, so a plate that reads HORIZON is entered as H0RIZ0N. The plate on the car doesn't change; only what you type does.
The plate has to match the car
Because the permit is checked by plate, a typo means the car on the street is not the car on the permit. Read the plate off the car, or a photo of it, and check it on the confirmation.
Your neighborhood may have its own guidance
Hours and signs differ from street to street, and some neighborhood associations publish their own parking instructions. If you're unsure, check with your association.

Who to contact

  • Questions about your permit account or the permit program: the City's Department of Transportation permit office, 404-330-6501 or DPW-Permits@atlantaga.gov.
  • Questions about a parking citation: ATLPlus, 404-201-5396.

A faster way: the GuestPermits app

If you create guest permits more than once or twice, retyping the same form gets old. GuestPermits is an iPhone and Android app that does the steps above for you, on your own City account. You enter your details once and save each guest's car once (a photo of the plate fills in the details). After that it's pick the car, pick the days, tap Create. The app opens the City's portal, fills in the form, enters 0 for O, checks that the amount due is $0.00, and submits. It also tells you when both guest slots are taken and the earliest date with room.

The City's guest permits are always free. The app is free to download and free for the first three permits it creates; after that a one-time $4.99 purchase removes the limit. Your information stays on your phone.
