Get GuestPermits

If you live in one of Atlanta's residential permit zones and you're tired of retyping your guests' cars into the City's portal, GuestPermits is on the App Store and Google Play. The City's guest permits are always free; the app is free for the first three permits it creates, and a one-time purchase removes the limit.

Get the app

iPhone
GuestPermits is on the App Store. Download GuestPermits for iPhone. If you tested through TestFlight, install the App Store version; the TestFlight build stops working 90 days after it was issued, and your saved details and vehicles stay on the phone.
Android
GuestPermits is on Google Play. Get GuestPermits on Google Play. If you joined the test track, the store version updates over it; nothing to uninstall.

What you need

  • An iPhone or Android phone.
  • An existing City of Atlanta residential parking permit account at atlparkingpermits.rmcpay.com. The app logs in as you; it has no account of its own.

What to expect

  • Setup takes about two minutes: your details as they appear on your permit, then a photo of each guest's plate. The app reads the plate, make, and color from the picture.
  • After that, creating a permit is pick the car, pick the days, tap Create. The app fills in and submits the City's own form on your account and shows you the confirmation.
  • Everything is stored on your phone. There is no account with us and no server holding your information. Details.
  • If something stops a permit, the app tells you what it was. A screenshot of that message is the most useful thing to send us.
