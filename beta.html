Help test GuestPermits

If you live in one of Atlanta's residential permit zones and you're tired of retyping your guests' cars into the City's portal, we'd like your help trying the app before it goes public. It's free during the beta, and your feedback shapes what ships.

What you need

  • An iPhone or Android phone.
  • An existing City of Atlanta residential parking permit account at atlparkingpermits.rmcpay.com. The app logs in as you; it has no account of its own.
  • A guest who parks at your place now and then, so there's something real to test.

Sign up

Leave your email and which phone you have. We'll send the install link and a short note on what to try. Nothing else is done with your address.

Sign up for the beta

Get the app

Android
Open this link on your Android phone and allow the install when Android asks: download GuestPermits for Android (beta build, September 9). We'll email you when a newer build is available.
iPhone
You'll get a TestFlight link. Install Apple's free TestFlight app first, then open the link. Updates arrive through TestFlight automatically.

What to expect

  • Setup takes about two minutes: your details as they appear on your permit, then a photo of each guest's plate. The app reads the plate, make, and color from the picture.
  • After that, creating a permit is pick the car, pick the days, tap Create. The app fills in and submits the City's own form on your account and shows you the confirmation.
  • Everything is stored on your phone. There is no account with us and no server holding your information. Details.
  • If something breaks, the app tells you what stopped it. Screenshots of that message are the most useful thing you can send.
